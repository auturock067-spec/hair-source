# HairSource V0.1 — verified research and publication copy

Reviewed **2026-10-29** (Asia/Shanghai). Scope: English-first bilingual procurement reference and a browser-local RFQ builder, with six practical hair-product groups. This is an educational information site, not a supplier verification service, medical service or legal opinion. Nothing below establishes stock, prices, MOQ, supplier relationships, testing or certification for PENG.

## Positioning and buyer job

Suggested public identity: **PENG Hair Sourcing** (provisional; no trademark-clearance claim). Suggested lead: **“Better specifications. More informed hair sourcing.”** Chinese: **“写清规格，让发制品采购更有依据。”**

Main job: help a salon, wig retailer or independent hair brand distinguish construction and fiber, specify an evaluation sample, and prepare a clear request before approaching a supplier. Buyer-friction hypotheses—unclear grades, inconsistent length/weight/color, sample-to-bulk mismatch and unsupported origin/ethical claims—are editorial inferences from the specification detail and claims emphasized by first-party sellers. No representative buyer interviews or market-size study were completed.

## Six sourcing groups and specification prompts

These are useful merchandising groups, not mutually exclusive technical classes: clip-ins and tape-ins can themselves be built with wefts. Treat the following as buying questions, not application instructions or universal standards.

| Group / Chinese | Concise publishable description | Require in a brief/sample approval |
|---|---|---|
| Wigs / 假发整头套 | Full-head units vary by cap construction, base, fiber, hairline and coverage. | Cap circumference/size and tolerance, lace/mono/PU/weft construction, lace dimensions/color, parting, hairline, knot treatment, length method, density definition, net hair weight versus total unit weight, fiber/processing and attachment. |
| Toppers / 局部补发片 | Partial-coverage pieces blend with existing hair; coverage is determined by base size and placement rather than length alone. | Base width × length and units, coverage/parting area, clip/tape interface, fiber, length, density/weight, shade swatch and base material. No medical treatment/guaranteed hair-loss claim. |
| Wefts / 发帘 | Hair attached along a continuous seam/spine, supplied in specified widths and weights. | Machine/hand-tied or other actual construction, usable width, seam thickness, net hair weight, hair-length distribution, cut-to-fit restrictions, shedding acceptance and finishing. Avoid proprietary supplier terms as generic certifications. |
| Clip-ins / 卡子接发 | Removable extension pieces using clips; set configuration matters as much as total weight. | Number of pieces, width/length of each, clips per piece, total and per-piece weight, clip materials/comfort/finish, texture and physical shade match. |
| Tape-ins / 胶片接发 | Extensions using an adhesive attachment interface; piece dimensions and tape specification must be agreed. | Tab width/height/thickness, net hair grams per piece or pair, pieces per pack, tape identity/ingredients documentation, removal/care guidance, replacement supplies and agreed sample evaluation. No universal hold time, reuse cycle or allergy-free promise. |
| Closures & frontals / 头顶封口片与前额发际线片 | Hair-bearing base pieces used to finish a style; a frontal generally covers a wider front hairline area than a smaller closure. | Exact base width × depth, lace/other material and shade, parting/ventilation area, knots/bleaching, density, length/weight, hairline finish and attachment compatibility. Dimensions and coverage must be specified; “HD lace” alone is insufficient. |

First-party terminology evidence, accessed 2026-10-29:

- [Jon Renau easiPart](https://jonrenau.com/toppers/beginning-stage/easipart/): describes a one-piece topper with human-hair and synthetic variants.
- [Jon Renau Top Blend specifications](https://jonrenau.com/top-blend/?setCurrencyId=1): separately identifies hair type, length and base dimensions. These are that seller's claims, not PENG inventory.
- [BELLAMI tape-in comparison](https://www.bellamihair.com/pages/learn-tape-in-hair-extensions): distinguishes tape constructions, including a tape/weft hybrid; confirms category overlap.
- [BELLAMI clip-in specification example](https://www.bellamihair.com/pages/nathalie-paris-hair-extensions-specifications): lists total grams, inches, piece count and clip configurations. The numeric figures are seller-specific examples, not recommended universal sizes.
- [Indique frontal](https://www.indiquehair.com/products/natural-wavy-frontal): identifies a frontal as providing more coverage than a standard closure.

## Fiber, grade and origin claims

**Publishable EN:** “Start with the fiber declaration: human hair, synthetic fiber, or a specified blend. Ask what each claim means, how it is verified, and whether the production lot matches your approved sample. Remy, virgin, raw, density percentages and letter grades should be defined in writing; a label alone is not proof of composition, processing history, lifespan or ethical origin.”

**中文：**“先确认纤维声明：真人发、化纤，或有明确比例的混合材质。让供应商说明每项声明的含义、验证依据，以及大货是否与确认样一致。Remy、virgin、raw、密度百分比和字母等级都应书面定义；名称本身不能证明成分、处理历史、使用寿命或伦理来源。”

Industry usage: Remy commonly refers to hair collected/kept with cuticles aligned root to tip; virgin commonly makes a claim about prior chemical processing. Neither term, by itself, proves donor origin or chain of custody. [BELLAMI's July 2026 explanation](https://www.bellamihair.com/a/blog/remy-v-non-remy) is first-party evidence of usage, not a binding international grade standard. Its description qualifies virgin by processing **at the source**; therefore do not assume colored finished products are wholly unprocessed. Ask separately about bleaching, dyeing, perming, steam/texturing, acid treatment and coatings at every relevant stage. No harmonized universal grading standard was established in this research.

Shade names/numbers are supplier systems; request a physical reference swatch, approved lighting and permitted lot variation. Agree whether length means stretched/straightened, longest strand or another method; specify units and tolerances. Separate advertised density from actual agreed fiber mass/distribution. For curly hair, texture and measurement method can change apparent length. Country-style names such as “Brazilian” must not be treated as documented collection origin without evidence.

Testing and sample plan (editorial recommendation): record sample/lot code and traceability; weigh and measure using agreed methods; assess color under controlled light, base construction and attachment dimensions; document washing/combing/shedding/tangling and any permitted styling conditions against agreed criteria. Use a qualified laboratory for relevant composition/chemical questions when warranted. A feel test, burn test or a single photo is not a definitive proof of human hair, Remy/virgin history or ethical sourcing; do not recommend unsafe informal burn testing.

## Procurement workflow and RFQ

1. Define product group, intended wearer/use, target market, fiber, construction and sizing.
2. Specify measurable acceptance: units, tolerances, per-piece/pack weight, color reference, texture, length method, base/attachment details and labeling.
3. Ask separately for sample fees/shipping, per-style/color/length MOQ and custom-packaging MOQ. PENG has no verified numeric offer or guaranteed low MOQ.
4. Approve a coded production-representative sample. Agree bulk-lot reservation, change approval, inspection/rejection and corrective-action terms.
5. Request traceability/claims evidence, material and processing documentation, applicable test scope and packaging/label review before purchase.
6. Compare itemized quote, currency, validity, production time, named Incoterm/place, freight/taxes, importer responsibility, warranty/returns and substitution rules.

RFQ fields: category; destination market; fiber/composition and claim requirements; construction; length/measurement method; density/weight; shade/texture; dimensions/attachments; quantity **with unit (pieces/sets/packs/kg as applicable)**; sample request; packaging/labeling; quality/traceability/tests; destination/delivery window; buyer email; optional company. Free-text consolidated specifications are acceptable in V0.1 if prompts expose these dimensions. Never use tea-specific kg-only assumptions for hair products.

Safe result copy: **“Your sourcing brief is ready. Nothing has been submitted. Download or copy it, then send it to your chosen supplier.”** / **“采购需求书已生成，尚未提交。请下载或复制后，自行发给所选供应商。”** No preset recipient or invented inbox. No server submission, automatic email, persistent RFQ PII or supplier response promise. Email draft must clearly require the user to add a real recipient, review and send it independently.

## Quality, ethics and US import starting points

**Publishable EN:** “Request a documented chain of custody for the actual lot, including collection/aggregation and processing locations, suppliers and transaction links. Assess consent, labor and sourcing claims with evidence appropriate to the risk. A supplier declaration or country label is not an independent ethical audit. Review current US forced-labor restrictions and entity lists before importing.”

**中文：**“要求提供对应批次的来源链文件，覆盖收集/归集、加工地点、供应商与交易衔接。根据风险核实自愿采集、劳动条件与来源声明。供应商自我声明或产地标签不等于独立伦理审计；进口前查阅美国最新强迫劳动限制与实体名单。”

US law prohibits covered goods made wholly or partly with forced labor. UFLPA creates a rebuttable presumption for goods made wholly/partly in Xinjiang or by a listed entity; it is not limited to the final assembly location. Do not portray every Chinese supplier as prohibited, or every shipment outside a named location as cleared. Seek qualified import advice for actual supply chains.

Primary sources accessed 2026-10-29:

- [CBP UFLPA explanation](https://www.help.cbp.gov/s/article/Article1842): scope and rebuttable presumption.
- [CBP Forced Labor Program FAQ](https://www.help.cbp.gov/s/article/Article-1725?language=en_US): enforcement/supporting-documentation context.
- [DHS 2025 strategy update](https://www.dhs.gov/sites/default/files/2025-08/25_0819_plcy_uflpa-strategy-2025-update-508.pdf): official strategy and entity-list maintenance.
- [DHS live entity-list entry point](https://www.dhs.gov/uflpa-entity-list): check current list for actual transactions; do not freeze a count in website copy.
- Historical official [DHS strategy](https://www.dhs.gov/sites/default/files/2022-06/22_0617_fletf_uflpa-strategy.pdf) records hair-sector enforcement context. This is history, not a current clearance determination for any supplier.

Access limit: main CBP forced-labor/UFLPA/news pages returned HTTP 403 in this research environment. The accessible CBP help and DHS official materials support the limited copy above; no current named-supplier screening was completed. Handle donor/person-level records securely; a public RFQ is not a place to upload sensitive donor identity documents.

**Truthful advertising:** Objective claims—human-hair percentage, origin, processing, heat resistance, lifespan, ethical sourcing, safety—need a reasonable evidentiary basis before publication. Disclaimers do not cure a misleading overall impression. [FTC advertising substantiation](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/ftc-policy-statement-regarding-advertising-substantiation), [FTC advertising FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business).

**Important labeling limit:** The FTC [Textile/Wool labeling guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/threading-your-way-through-labeling-requirements-under-textile-wool-acts) explicitly lists **wigs** as not covered by the Textile Act guidance. Do not say all wigs require the standard Textile Act fiber label. Exact construction/material and other laws, truthful description, customs-origin marking and state rules still require product-specific review. The page notes separate Wool Act considerations. No complete hair-product labeling/legal classification was established here.

## EU, UK, Canada and Australia

**EU EN:** “Determine the rules for the exact product and intended consumer use. Where GPSR applies, address safety assessment, technical documentation, traceability, required economic-operator details and online-offer information. Review relevant REACH substance restrictions and article-information duties for the actual materials, coatings, dyes, bases and attachments. No generic ‘REACH certified’ or CE badge replaces this assessment.”

**中文：**“按具体产品及消费者用途判断适用规则。适用GPSR时，应落实安全评估、技术资料、追溯、责任经济运营者信息与线上销售信息要求。按材质、涂层、染料、底网及接头核查REACH限制与物品信息义务。通用的‘REACH认证’或CE标记不能替代适用性评估。”

- [EU GPSR consolidated regulation, current 29 May 2026 text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/2026-05-29): consumer-product scope and relationship to specific legislation; check manufacturer/importer/responsible-operator and distance-sales provisions as relevant. A B2B sale in the supply chain does not automatically remove a consumer-intended product from scope. Exclusively professional products not reasonably likely to reach consumer use require a distinct assessment.
- [Commission GPSR Q&A](https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/safety/consumers/consumers_safety_gate/obligationsForBusinesses/documents/Q%26A.pdf): B2B/consumer-use distinction.
- [ECHA restricted substances](https://echa.europa.eu/information-restricted-substances), [ECHA restrictions table](https://echa.europa.eu/substances-restricted-under-reach?p=1): restrictions depend on specific entries/conditions, not a universal test panel for every hair product.
- [ECHA Candidate List in articles](https://echa.europa.eu/regulations/reach/candidate-list-substances-in-articles): article obligations can be triggered by relevant SVHC content. No particular hair lot or attachment composition is verified here.

**UK:** Distinguish Great Britain from Northern Ireland. Check product safety, records/traceability, labeling and incident/recall duties; do not apply EU rules identically to all UK routes. [OPSS business guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/product-safety-advice-for-businesses) (updated July 2026), [GB GPSR guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/general-product-safety-regulations-2005/general-product-safety-regulations-2005-great-britain). The guidance explains EU GPSR applies in NI from December 2024. Adhesives, cosmetics and therapeutic/medical claims may change the legal analysis and are outside this basic product-reference scope.

**Canada:** Where the goods are consumer products in scope, check CCPSA safety obligations, applicable regulations, traceability and incident reporting. Do not assume one foreign test report authorizes sale. [Health Canada general prohibitions](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/consumer-product-safety/legislation-guidelines/guidelines-policies/information-regulated-parties-enforcement-approach-general-prohibitions.html), [industry incident reporting](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/consumer-product-safety/report-incident/industry.html). Product/label language, customs and claims need separate exact-product review.

**Australia:** Check relevant mandatory standards/bans, safe supply, evidence for claims and incident/recall obligations. Not all goods have a dedicated mandatory standard; that does not make unsafe or misleading supply acceptable. [ACCC product-safety rules](https://www.productsafety.gov.au/business/understand-product-safety-rules/product-safety-standards-and-how-to-comply), [laws and liability](https://www.productsafety.gov.au/business/understand-product-safety-rules/product-safety-laws-and-liability). Check customs/biosecurity for the actual processed human-hair or synthetic product and accessories before import; this research did not establish a universal hair import permit/exemption.

All destination sources accessed 2026-10-29. This is a starting-point guide, not exhaustive product compliance or legal advice. No claimed destination compliance for an actual supplier/product is supported.

## PetSupplyHub compliance addendum — ordinary accessories only

Scope: non-food, non-drug ordinary accessories. Exclude food, supplements, veterinary medicines, therapeutic claims and electrical/chemical specialist goods unless separately assessed. Pet safety and human consumer safety are related but not identical; do not imply a universal pet-product approval regime.

**US EN:** “Identify the rules that apply to the actual accessory, materials, intended users and claims. CPSC certification duties arise where applicable rules require them; there is no universal CPSIA certificate for every pet product. Ask for relevant test evidence and lot traceability, and review recalls and hazards.”

**中文：**“按具体配饰、材质、用途与声明判断适用规则。CPSC认证义务取决于是否涉及相应法规，不存在覆盖所有宠物用品的通用CPSIA证书。要求提供相关检测依据与批次追溯，并核查召回和风险。”

[CPSC general-use certification/testing](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Testing-Certification/General-Use-Products-Certification-and-Testing) ties general certificates to applicable rules; child-product rules depend on intended child use, not just the presence of a pet theme. Its page also flags current eFiling requirements for most **regulated** imports from July 2026; do not extend that to unregulated products without assessment. Review current entry mechanics for actual imports rather than hardcoding a universal rule in evergreen copy.

FTC's [Textile/Wool guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/threading-your-way-through-labeling-requirements-under-textile-wool-acts) lists dog coats, other pet clothing and pet furniture as not covered under that Textile Act guidance. This is not an exemption from every labeling, safety, origin or Wool Act obligation.

**EU scope:** GPSR covers consumer-intended/foreseeably consumer-used products where not displaced by specific EU safety rules and relevant risk coverage; it excludes food/feed, medicinal products, living animals and other listed categories. Ordinary accessories may be in scope, but determine exact product/material and risks first. Do not claim GPSR protects all animal-only injury risks as though it were a universal animal-welfare certification. Food-contact uses, animal-derived materials, chemicals or other special features can require separate rules. Use the [GPSR regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/2026-05-29), not a blanket “all pet supplies are GPSR certified” statement.

Practical buying prompts (recommendations): animal species/size/behavior, fit ranges, load and attachment expectations with evidence, seams/sharp edges, small detachable parts, entrapment/chewing risks, material and cleaning instructions, intended indoor/outdoor use, warnings and batch identification. “Indestructible”, “non-toxic”, “escape-proof”, crash-protection and guaranteed load claims need supporting evidence; do not publish them generically.

**Carriers and airline claims:** “Confirm the exact carrier dimensions, animal fit and current acceptance requirements with the operating airline for the route, aircraft and travel mode before purchase. An airline acceptance decision is not guaranteed by a generic product label.” / “购买前按具体航线、机型和运输方式，向承运航空公司确认箱包尺寸、宠物适配及最新接收要求；通用标签不能保证航空公司接收。”

[IATA container guidance](https://www.iata.org/en/publications/newsletters/iata-knowledge-hub/step-by-step-instructions-on-how-to-select-the-correct-container-for-an-animal-being-transported-by-air/) explicitly says IATA does not certify, approve or endorse any particular container. [IATA pet travel guidance](https://www.iata.org/en/programs/cargo/live-animals/pets/) directs travelers to confirm actual acceptance with the airline. Do not display “IATA certified” or universal “airline approved” badges. All Pet addendum sources accessed 2026-10-29.
